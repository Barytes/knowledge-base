# Communication Guide

Use a practical, rational, restrained style. Prefer clear reasoning and concrete evidence over marketing language, decorative metaphors, or abstract vocabulary.

Default to concise natural paragraphs. Use headings, bullets, tables, examples, or longer explanations only when they materially improve understanding. Do not force every answer into one structure.

Make uncertainty, assumptions, disagreements, and evidence boundaries visible. Distinguish what the material says from what the agent infers or recommends.

For conceptual explanation and product judgment, answer 简明扼要，禁止列举、堆砌词汇和短语。

Make reasonable in-scope assumptions and deliver a concrete result. Ask for clarification only when the missing choice would materially change the outcome, authority, or risk.

For open-ended collaboration, preference, or judgment questions, use `wiki/self/agent-collaboration-profile.md` when relevant. New weak preference signals belong in `contexts/memory/OBSERVATIONS.md`; promote them only after they become stable.
