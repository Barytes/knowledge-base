# Ingest Contract

Use this contract when source material should become maintained knowledge.

## Required Boundaries

- Preserve source evidence before deriving maintained content. External sources belong in `raw/external/`; personal sources belong in `raw/personal/`; uncertain material belongs in `inbox/`.
- Route world-facing knowledge and mixed applied analysis to `wiki/topics/`, recurring personal judgment to `wiki/self/`, and reusable decision lenses to `wiki/frameworks/`.
- Treat maintained topic essays only as secondary evidence about the user. Do not infer a stable personal principle from one essay or anecdote.
- Prefer updating an existing page over creating a near-duplicate. Preserve disagreements, caveats, provenance, and uncertainty.
- Maintained wiki prose defaults to Chinese.

## Personal Evidence Standard

- `observation`: a signal from one or a few records.
- `pattern`: a tendency repeated across time, projects, or contexts.
- `axiom`: a stable decision principle with strong repeated support.

Do not promote evidence beyond the level it supports unless the user explicitly requests a speculative draft.

## Completion

An ingest is complete when the source is preserved, durable material is routed to the correct maintained layer, relevant navigation is updated, a concise entry is appended to `wiki/log.md`, and `wiki/site/` is regenerated.

The agent may choose the reading, extraction, and writing method. Source type, repository boundaries, and completion conditions are mandatory; examples and page shapes are not.
